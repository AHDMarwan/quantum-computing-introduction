## Policy

When a term is overloaded in the literature, this repository states its local convention explicitly. In particular:

- **PQC** means a parameterized quantum circuit as a computational object.
- **VQA** means a hybrid variational optimization framework.
- **VQC** is reserved, when possible, for a variational quantum classifier and is expanded on first use.
- **QNN** is treated as architecture-dependent terminology rather than as a unique mathematical object.

The broad vocabulary is aligned where possible with **ISO/IEC 4879:2024, Information technology — Quantum computing — Vocabulary**.
