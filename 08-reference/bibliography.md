## Suggested reading order

For a first pass:

1. Nielsen & Chuang or Preskill for foundations;
2. Watrous for quantum information theory;
3. Shor, Grover, and QPE material for canonical algorithms;
4. Cerezo et al. for VQAs;
5. Biamonte et al. for QML overview;
6. Havlíček et al., Huang et al., and Caro et al. for modern QML questions about features, data, advantage, and generalization.
