Quantum Computing Introduction

Notes on quantum information, computational models, hardware platforms, quantum algorithms, variational quantum algorithms, quantum information theory, and quantum machine learning.

Foundations Repository index
Single-qubit pure state
|ψ⟩ = α|0⟩ + β|1⟩

The amplitudes satisfy |α|² + |β|² = 1. Measurement probabilities depend on the chosen basis.

About

Scope

The material is organized as educational chapters. Mathematical foundations, computational models, physical hardware, algorithms, and learning methods are treated as separate but related topics.

No previous knowledge of quantum computing is assumed. Familiarity with linear algebra, complex numbers, probability, and basic calculus is useful.
